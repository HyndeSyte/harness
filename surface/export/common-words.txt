# Common English words a capitalised token may be without counting as a name (surface/README.md,
# the proper-noun gate). One a line, lower case; `#` for a note line. Function words, pronouns,
# common imperative and ordinary verbs. No names: words that are also common first names or
# surnames (name-words.txt: may, june, will, mark, grace, rose, cook, post and the like) are left
# out, and a test keeps the two lists apart. Months and weekdays are not here: the gate lets one
# stand only next to a number or a date (lib.ts, CALENDAR_WORDS). The owner's config adds more
# (allow_words).
