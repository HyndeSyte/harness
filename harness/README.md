# Harness

The calendar specialist behind Cain on Discord, Claude and GPT as engines, and a small
deterministic controller that decides everything. Stage S1. Once the calendar_changes option is on,
it changes events on iCloud Family and your Gmail calendar when you ask: a clear change is
done at once with a receipt and Undo, an unclear one is asked once by name, and anything that
looks pasted gets a tap. You can also say what a Family event means for you, and Gmail and work
follow it.

See the Documentation tab for set-up and commands.
